Privacy & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

ErgoEase is committed to using submitted information responsibly and transparently.

Last Updated

June 2026

Information We Collect

ErgoEase may collect information submitted through ergonomic screening forms, employer intake forms, contact forms, and related workflows. This may include names, email addresses, employer or organization names, role or occupation information, assessment responses, and report-related information.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to generate individual reports, employer summary reports, equipment recommendations, cost estimates, communication emails, and related ErgoEase services.

Employer and Team Reports

For team screenings, individual assessment responses may be summarized in aggregate to create employer-level workforce reports. These reports are intended to identify general ergonomic trends, risk areas, and planning-level recommendations.

Third-Party Services

ErgoEase may use third-party services such as Google Forms, Google Sheets, Google Drive, Gmail, Stripe, GitHub Pages, and other tools necessary to operate the platform, process payments, generate reports, and deliver communications.

Payment Information

Payments are processed through Stripe. ErgoEase does not directly store full credit card numbers or payment card details.

Data Sharing

ErgoEase does not sell personal information. Information may be shared only as necessary to operate the service, generate reports, process payments, communicate results, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Data Security

ErgoEase uses reasonable administrative and technical practices to protect submitted information. However, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be directed to ergoease.app@gmail.com.
